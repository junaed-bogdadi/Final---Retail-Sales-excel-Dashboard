# Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard** created using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides a complete overview of retail business performance by analyzing sales, profit, quantity, orders, product categories, sub-categories, regions, customer segments, and yearly performance.

The main objective of this dashboard is to help business users quickly understand sales performance, identify profitable categories, track monthly trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- Year
- Region
- Segment

These filters allow users to analyze business performance by specific time periods, regions, and customer segments.

---

### 2. Sales by District

A map visualization shows sales distribution across different districts/divisions of Bangladesh, including Dhaka, Rajshahi, Khulna, Rangpur, Sylhet, and others.

This helps identify which geographic areas are contributing more to total sales.

---

### 3. Top 5 Sub-Category Sales

The dashboard shows the top-performing product sub-categories based on sales.

Top sub-categories include:

- Phones
- Storage
- Binders

Phones generated the highest sales, making it one of the strongest product sub-categories in the business.

---

### 4. Category-wise Sales

The dashboard includes a category-wise sales chart to compare the performance of major product categories.

The main categories analyzed are:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

This section helps understand which product category contributes the most to total sales.

---

### 5. Monthly Sales vs Profit

The dashboard compares monthly sales and profit from January to December.

From the chart, sales increased strongly in the later months of the year, especially around September, November, and December. This indicates better business performance during the final quarter.

---

### 6. Category-wise Profit

The dashboard uses a waterfall chart to show profit contribution by category.

Approximate profit values:

| Category | Profit |
|---|---:|
| Furniture | 18K |
| Office Supplies | 122K |
| Technology | 145K |
| Grand Total | 286K |

Technology generated the highest profit, followed by Office Supplies. Furniture contributed the lowest profit among the three categories.

---

## Key Insights

- Total sales reached approximately **2.3 million**.
- Total profit was around **286K**.
- The business achieved a **12.5% profit margin**.
- Technology was the most profitable category.
- Phones were one of the highest-selling sub-categories.
- Sales performance improved strongly toward the end of the year.
- The dashboard allows users to filter performance by year, region, and customer segment.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard insights, the following recommendations can be made:

1. Focus more on Technology products, as this category generates the highest profit.
2. Increase marketing and inventory planning for high-performing sub-categories such as Phones and Storage.
3. Investigate why Furniture has lower profit contribution compared to other categories.
4. Use regional insights to improve sales strategy in underperforming districts.
5. Strengthen promotional campaigns during the months with lower sales performance.
6. Continue tracking sales and profit together to ensure revenue growth is also profitable.

---

## Conclusion

This Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of business performance. It helps users understand sales trends, profit contribution, product category performance, and regional sales distribution.

The project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used as an effective business intelligence tool for retail data analysis and decision-making.
