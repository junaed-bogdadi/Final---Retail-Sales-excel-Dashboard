# Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

An interactive retail sales dashboard built in Microsoft Excel. It brings sales, profit, quantity, orders, and profit margin into one view so users can explore performance by year, region, and customer segment.

## What the Dashboard Shows

- **KPI cards:** Sales, profit, quantity, orders, and profit margin
- **Interactive filters:** Year, region, and customer segment
- **Sales by district:** Geographic view of sales across Bangladesh
- **Top 5 sub-categories:** Comparison of the highest-selling product sub-categories
- **Category-wise sales:** Sales contribution from Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology
- **Monthly sales vs profit:** Comparison across January to December
- **Category-wise profit:** Profit contribution by product category

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Slicers

- Map, bar, column, pie, and waterfall charts
- Dashboard formatting and data visualization

## How to Use

1. [Download the Excel dashboard](Final%20-%20Retail%20Sales%20Dashboard.xlsx).
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel.
3. Go to the dashboard sheet.
4. Use the **Year**, **Region**, and **Segment** slicers to explore the results.
5. Clear slicer selections to return to the overall view.

## Example Observations

In the overall dashboard view, Technology has the highest category profit, followed by Office Supplies. Phones appears among the leading sub-categories by sales. The monthly chart also shows relatively strong sales in several later months of the year.

These are descriptive observations from the dashboard. Further analysis would be needed to determine *why* performance differs between categories or months.
